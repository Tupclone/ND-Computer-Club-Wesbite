We send out weekly e-mail updates about upcoming events, workshops and announce guest speaker lineups throughout the semesters. Any upcoming events will be updated through our weekly email and this website, as well as our Instagram.

Our meetings are open to everyone!

We are always open to collaboration — whether you are an ACMS, first year, or another student organisation, please don’t hesitate to reach out!

If you have any questions, or would like to contribute to the club, feel free to email us at CSE.cseclub.1@nd.edu
